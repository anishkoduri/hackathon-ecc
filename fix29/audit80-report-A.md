## Findings

### 80-A1 — BLOCKING: `design-taste-frontend` dispatches implementation work with no routing — OWED-29-3 is wrong for this skill

**Evidence.**

- `skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md:455`: "**Redesign of an existing page (keeping content + wiring).** When the job is a full-page redesign that keeps every fact, link, form, field, handler, route, id, and test hook, follow the Hallmark redesign flow (`references/hallmark/references/redesign.md`)"
- `skills/design-taste-frontend/references/hallmark/references/redesign.md:68`: "Past three pages, give each page to its own subagent where the harness has them, with that tokens file, the page's files and this file."
- The same file's `Files` block (redesign.md:55–60, e.g. "Files · edit: app/page.tsx, app/pricing/page.tsx, app/globals.css · create: app/tokens.css, app/base.css") shows what those page subagents do: they **edit and create project files** — restyle/full redesign implementation, not review.

**Why it matters.** The manifest (§2b) excludes `design-taste-frontend` as "Dispatches a fresh-context review sub-agent (read-only hero/finish review); design direction is done inline by the design-lead", and OWED-29-3 records all three challenged skills as carrying "no implementation dispatch". That is true of the hero/finish review (SKILL.md:539, gates.md:243–245 — read-only, out of scope), but false of the redesign flow: redesign.md:68 is a live, unconditional implementation fan-out (one subagent per page, past three pages) with no director brief, no NO-GUESSING rule, no verify-and-merge step, no cost-aware justification, and no partition rule — and it is worse than an unrouted dispatch, because the pages **share mutable state** (one stylesheet, `docs/design-tokens.md`, `tokens.css`/`base.css`), so FACTORY-DISPATCH_F29's "partition by file/module boundary so no two drones share files" is violated by construction unless the briefs serialize the shared files. The routing set is therefore 10 skills, not 9. `fix29.py`'s module docstring repeats the false claim ("design-taste-frontend dispatch parallel/review subagents but no implementation work") and must be corrected with the fix.

**Fix.** Add a factory routing pointer in `redesign.md` (or in `design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md:455` where the flow is invoked): each page subagent gets a director brief (goal, the page's files in scope and explicitly out of scope, constraints, acceptance criteria, verification steps, what to return); the shared stylesheet/tokens files are single-writer (locked once, before page fan-out, or written by one named drone); NO-GUESSING_F29 and VERIFY-MERGE_F29 apply; fan-out past 3 pages is justified in one line (COST-AWARE_F29). Update MANIFEST §2 (move `design-taste-frontend` to the routing set, routing set 9 → 10), fix29.py's docstring, and the fix29.py census guard's in-scope list.

### 80-A2 — BLOCKING: NO-GUESSING reconciliation is incomplete — the "pick the reasonable option" sentence survives uncarved in the dispatch template and in `orch-build-mvp`

**Evidence.** Four occurrences of the Belief-in-the-brief sentence exist in the built tree. Two were carved (manifest §2c(1) is accurate for these two); two were not:

- CARVED — `skills/orch-pipeline/SKILL.md:72`: "…if a detail is ambiguous, they pick the reasonable option and note it, they don't stall. **Bounded by NO-GUESSING (fix #29, NO-GUESSING_F29):** 'pick the reasonable option' applies only to carrying out the briefed approach…"
- CARVED — `skills/feature-expansion/SKILL.md:150`: same sentence + same carve-out.
- **UNCARVED — `skills/orchestrate/references/dispatch-prompt.md:35`**: "**Belief in the brief:** every brief opens with the goal and the belief that it is achievable — the work is doable, the plan is sound, build it. Implementers trust the brief and build it — they do not second-guess the plan, hedge, or wait for reassurance; they commit to the approach and make it work; if a detail is ambiguous, they pick the reasonable option and note it, they don't stall." The same uncarved sentence repeats in the fast-worker template preamble in the same file ("if a detail is ambiguous, pick the reasonable option and note it, don't stall").
- **UNCARVED — `skills/orch-build-mvp/SKILL.md:70`**: "…**Belief in the brief:** implementers trust the brief and build it — they do not second-guess the plan, hedge, or wait for reassurance; they commit to the approach and make it work; if a detail is ambiguous, they pick the reasonable option and note it, they don't stall." (No carve-out follows.)

**Why it matters.** `dispatch-prompt.md:1–8` states it is "the authoritative templates for every delegation" and orchestrate `SKILL.md:227` orders "Compose every dispatch from the templates in dispatch-prompt.md". The template is the **drone-facing text**: every implementer brief composed from it still instructs the drone to guess ("pick the reasonable option") with no NO-GUESSING boundary, so the carve-outs in `orch-pipeline` and `feature-expansion` (orchestrator-side files the drone never reads) do not reach the drone. The template is also internally self-contradictory without the boundary: the fast-worker preamble says both "pick the reasonable option" and "If a real design decision surfaces, stop and return NEEDS_CONTEXT naming it — do not invent an answer", with no rule saying which wins. A second, unreconciled tension sits in the same file: the "First-build carve-out" ("executors do NOT return `NEEDS_CONTEXT` for a missing user-resolvable fact or an under-specified point — they state the assumption (the safe/reversible option) and proceed") directly overrides NO-GUESSING_F29 for under-specified points during first builds, and the manifest never addresses it. `orch-build-mvp:70` is the same defect in the standalone pipeline's step 4 (the GAN loop drives each slice there), so the guessing instruction survives on both engines. The manifest §2c(1) claim that the conflict was "reconciled" is therefore overstated: it was reconciled in 2 of 4 occurrences, missing the two that execute.

**Fix.** (a) Add the identical NO-GUESSING carve-out sentence to `dispatch-prompt.md:35` (skeleton Belief line) and to the fast-worker template preamble, using the same HOW-vs-WHAT boundary wording as orch-pipeline:72. (b) Add the carve-out to `orch-build-mvp/SKILL.md:70`. (c) Reconcile the first-build carve-out in `dispatch-prompt.md` with NO-GUESSING_F29 in writing: state-the-assumption-and-proceed applies only to carrying out a briefed approach (HOW), never to a decision the brief leaves unspecified (WHAT — file, scope call, acceptance criterion), which is returned as a question. `fix29.py` §1's anchor assertion (`count=1` on orch-pipeline only) is what let the other two copies slip through; extend the reconciliation to a tree-wide grep assertion for the sentence.
