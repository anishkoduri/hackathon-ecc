## Findings

### 82-A1 — MINOR: the 81-A8 scoping decision stands but its recorded justification is thin, and the step-0 wording stays spawn-shaped

**Evidence.** `commands/gan-build.md:26` (step 0, cold start): "dispatch to the design skill for that surface — `design-taste-frontend` for a landing page, `ui-ux-pro-max` for a dashboard/app surface — on model `grok-4.7-high`, first to generate the direction through the package skills (per the design-package rule) and record it in `docs/design-tokens.md`, then launch the planner with that recorded direction." MANIFEST §2(b) records the decision as orchestrator-level design-lead work, citing `orchestrate:31` and the design package being out of scope. Contrast the planner launch one step later (`gan-build.md:27`): "Launch the `gan-planner` agent (`agents/ecc-gan-planner.md`) as a Cursor subagent" — explicit about the spawn form. Step 0 never says whether "dispatch to the design skill" means invoking the skill inline as the design-lead (the orchestrator acting in its design role) or spawning a subagent on `grok-4.7-high`.

**Why it matters.** If a future reader executes step 0 as a spawned subagent — the natural reading of "dispatch … on model X" in a file where every other launch names a subagent — that spawn writes `docs/design-tokens.md` with no director brief, no NO-GUESSING rule, and no verification step, and its target on the dashboard path (`ui-ux-pro-max`) carries no factory routing at all. The decision as recorded does not rule that reading out. Minor, not blocking: the orchestrator-role reading is the better one (design-lead is the orchestrator's own model and role; direction generation is a big decision governed by DESIGNPKG-AUTO; cold-start only; downstream use verified by step 3a), and the revisit trigger is recorded — but a scoping decision that leaves the execution form ambiguous is an incomplete edge.

**Fix.** Either (a) strengthen the MANIFEST §2(b) record to pin the reading — step 0 invokes the design-lead inline as the orchestrator, not a spawned drone; if executed as a spawned subagent it must carry a director brief (goal, files in/out of scope, constraints, acceptance criteria, verification steps, what to return) — and add a parenthetical to that effect in `gan-build.md:26`; or (b) extend the `gan-build.md:12` pointer with one clause covering the step-0 design-direction dispatch as a single-task factory drone with a director brief. Option (a) is the smaller change and matches the decision already recorded.

### 82-A2 — MINOR: the B2 planner-spec correction cap is set at one with no stated reason — stricter than the plugin's established 2-round fix loops

**Evidence.** `commands/gan-build.md:30` (step 3a): "If the spec fails the check, the planner is re-dispatched with a correction brief naming the failed criterion and the required correction, distinct from the original brief (at most one correction re-dispatch; if the spec still fails, stop and report the failure with evidence)." MANIFEST §2(c)(5) records the cap as "this fix's choice, bounding the loop per the plugin's no-unbounded-loops convention; the auditor specified no cap."

**Why it matters.** A cap is justified — the plugin's no-unbounded-loops convention requires *a* cap — but the convention justifies a cap, not the value *one*. The plugin's established fix-loop cap is two rounds: `skills/feature-expansion/SKILL.md:158` ("at most 2 rounds"), `skills/orch-pipeline/SKILL.md:74` ("capped 2-round fix loop (max 3 rounds total)"), and the same 2-round cap in the orchestrate escalation ladder's "No third mechanical retry" pattern. The planner-spec cap of one is stricter than all of them with no stated reason, and it is asymmetric with the generator correction path in the same file, where a correction re-dispatch consumes an iteration within `max_iterations` (up to 15). A planner spec that fails once may fail on a correctable detail (a missing acceptance criterion, a mistranslated scope line) that a second correction would fix; stopping after one turns a recoverable miss into a stopped run. Minor because the failure path is safe (stop and report with evidence, not silent acceptance) and the loop is bounded either way.

**Fix.** Either (a) record the reason one suffices — e.g. a planner spec that fails twice means the brief/spec is wrong, not the planner, so the orchestrator must rework the brief rather than retry the drone — in MANIFEST §2(c)(5) and as a parenthetical in `gan-build.md:30`; or (b) align the cap to the plugin's 2-round convention ("at most two correction re-dispatches"). Option (a) is the smaller change; either closes the unjustified-value gap. (If (b) is chosen, the skill mirror lines and verify29's planner-spec assertion should be checked for a cap value — the current verifier asserts the step's presence, not the number.)
