## New findings

### 85-B1 — MINOR (tree) — redesign paragraph's correction-brief path does not name the failed criterion

**Evidence.**

- `skills/design-taste-frontend/references/hallmark/references/redesign.md:68` (factory routing paragraph): "the orchestrator verifies every page drone's output against the brief's acceptance criteria and sends failed output back with a correction brief (VERIFY-MERGE_F29)". The correction brief is unadorned — it never says the brief names the failed criterion (or the required correction).
- Every other drone-output consumption site names it: orchestrate escalation ladder step 1 (`SKILL.md:252`, "The re-dispatch carries a correction brief naming the failed acceptance criterion and the required correction, distinct from the original brief"), orch-pipeline Phase 5 Review (`SKILL.md:74`, same construction sentence), feature-expansion fix loop (`SKILL.md:158`, same sentence), gan-style-harness factory paragraph (`SKILL.md:14`, "failed output goes back with a correction brief naming the failed criterion"), gan-build ORCHESTRATOR-CHECK (`commands/gan-build.md:61`, "re-dispatched with a correction brief naming the failed criterion and the required correction") and planner-spec check 3a (`:30`, same). The redesign paragraph is the only consumption site where the naming clause is missing.
- The verifier cannot catch this: 7c/10i assert the exact construction sentence in feature-expansion, orch-pipeline, and orchestrate only — nothing asserts a naming clause for redesign.

**Why it matters.** VERIFY-MERGE's bite is that the re-dispatched drone knows exactly what failed; a correction brief that does not name the failed criterion leaves the drone to guess what to fix — the failure mode the mechanism exists to prevent. The verify step itself is present and against acceptance criteria, so the criterion is identified at verification time; what is missing is one clause carrying it into the re-dispatch. Bounded, one-clause gap.

**Fix.** Add the naming clause to the redesign sentence ("sends failed output back with a correction brief naming the failed acceptance criterion and the required correction, distinct from the original brief") and add a verify29 assertion for it mirroring the feature-expansion / orch-pipeline / orchestrate construction checks. Tree + verifier change; no other file needs to move.
